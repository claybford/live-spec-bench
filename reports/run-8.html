Apparatus and limits

  • The canary confirmed the pinned model and variant, a writable repo, a refused write to the bound methodology file, and an invisible evidence directory.
  • The first launch died before any session: the inhibitor re-exec handed systemd-inhibit the bare driver.sh, which it resolves through PATH. Fixed and relaunched (watch-reexec); the failed launch ran no session, and the second ran all 18.
  • Scoring exposed a probe defect: answers() let another described item's answer stand in for the plant's. The run's evidence was re-checked with the fixture passage id (no session re-run), and the G6 table pairs the plant only (watch-answerpair).
  • The planted passage was staged uncommitted and cut in the working tree; it appears in no commit. The handoff spec is the authority (rubric G).
  • The P2 sessions legitimately updated their target values (ae86 2.5→1.8 g-mm; factorytax 90→120 days); the planted dependent, status clause and requirement passage stayed byte-identical, and all five core sections survived.
  • One grader-summary disagreement: it attributed a derived item to factorytax-1; the transcript has none (the clause was fixed in the same commit). G2's outcome is unaffected.
  • Probe scope only: no baseline arm, no held-out pair, no A–F. Enforcement, one-at-a-time answering and the watched/decided questions are not re-tested (dl-probe).

Bottom line
