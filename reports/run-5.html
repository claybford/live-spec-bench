Caveats

  • Held-out O9/O10 skipped. F1–F3's held-out clauses are N/A; the deck remains the one the rules were tuned on (dl-heldout).
  • W1 never ran. The driver's eligibility test greps for rel="depends-on" and matches the seed's own worked example, so factorytax-1 — zero edges, its own start printing DEPENDS-ON EDGES: 0 — was wrongly skipped. Harness defect, recorded as watch-w1grep; W1 is unexercised this run (dl-w1).
  • No cell split. B1/B3/B4 and C12 are N/A; zero splits across six method cells is itself a result, and the factorytax cells held their operations manuals in main without stating why (C3 PARTIAL).
  • Dirty handoffs are the headline on both arms: ae86-1 and factorytax-3 (method) and all six baselines end with uncommitted work. Baseline F4 is structurally hard: no baseline session commits after its inst commit (factorytax) or after O5 (ae86).
  • C6's instrument/seed divergence persists: the rubric names the second overflow and the leap-day repeat as recurrences; all six seeds call the diagnosis unconfirmed, so only 1 of 6 promoted a diagnostic row.
